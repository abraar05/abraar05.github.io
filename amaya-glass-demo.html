Methodology

How the ledger is built

Deduplicate repeated IMEI appearances into one defensible movement record per device, then validate state and citation integrity.

1

Ingest sources

Normalize tab, row, date, model, and citation from every source workbook.

2

Deduplicate IMEI

Collapse every occurrence of an IMEI into one row, sorted by identifier.

3

Resolve state

Classify terminal state from latest dated event; flag never-classified devices.

4

Detect conflicts

Flag terminal-state disagreement, model mismatch, and date inversion.

5

Review queue

Prioritize conflicts by severity and surface them before reporting.
